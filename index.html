04 / Analytical method

How the model produces its results.

The calculation separates existing bookings, expected future demand, assumed prices and costs. Material assumptions and limitations are documented below.

Illustrative finding
+$40,800

Highest modeled incremental contribution

In the Antarctic Discovery sample, a $25,000 targeted marketing increase with an assumed 25% pickup lift outperforms the other illustrated choices. But projected occupancy only reaches 81.7%—still below the 85% target.

This is not a recommendation to approve spending. The pickup lift, acquisition economics and available inventory would need validation with historical data.

How the demonstration works

Inputs → calculations → commercial impact

  • Inputs: hypothetical booking snapshots, future pickup, fares, capacity, ancillary revenue and operating costs.
  • Calculations: projected guests, occupancy, core and ancillary revenue, and a simplified contribution proxy.
  • Business impact: comparison against base-case revenue, contribution and occupancy, alongside the evidence needed before implementing a change.
  • Controls: editable assumptions, visible source table, export/import, and documented limitations.
Additional technical detail · optional
What real data would be needed to make this operational?

Historical booking snapshots by departure date and days-before-sailing; actual berth/cabin availability; fare classes and transactions; cancellations and refunds; channel-level marketing spend and attribution; ancillary attach rates and fulfillment costs; competitor itinerary comparability; FX effects; and Revenue Management-approved KPI and cost definitions.

Each source would need a named owner, refresh frequency, quality checks, and documented reconciliations before forecasts were used for decisions.

How would this scale into an enterprise BI workflow?

Define the source tables and data contract with the BI owner; validate field types, keys, booking snapshots, and financial definitions; transform data with approved tools (potentially Power Query); model measures and relationships; then build governed reporting in Power BI or the company's existing stack. Add a controlled refresh and review process, with evidence for each material assumption.

This is a proposed implementation approach—not a claim of previous hands-on Power BI / Power Query employment experience.

What are the most important model limitations?

Bookings and revenue are invented; there is one fictitious current observation and a designed target curve, not a historical booking-pace dataset. The assumed extra demand from marketing and discounts is not estimated statistically. Capacity is simplified to guest spaces rather than cabin class. The model excludes actual cancellation/refund timing, yield optimization, departure costs, taxes and many operational constraints.

The result labeled contribution is a scenario-comparison proxy, not audited accounting profit or a forecast of Quark's actual results. AI assisted prototype implementation; its calculations still require independent validation.
